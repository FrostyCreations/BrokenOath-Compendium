Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the site at broked-db.pages.dev ("the Broken Oath Compendium", formerly "Broked DB", "we", "the site") stores, why, and the choices you have. The site is run by an individual in South Africa, so the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) applies. We also follow the main principles of the EU and UK GDPR for visitors from those regions, and the US state privacy rights described below.

What happens to your save files

When you load a save file or a saves folder, the file is read entirely inside your browser. It is never sent to us or anyone else. The planner turns it into a build (class, level, gear, passive tree, gems) on your own device.

What we store, and only when you choose to publish

Please don't put personal information (real names, emails, contact details) into names or notes. They are published as you type them.

Stored on your own device

The site keeps your settings and work in your browser's local storage: things like your last opened build, filter rules, fight settings, which tab you were on, and the keys that let you update or remove what you published. This never leaves your device unless you publish something. Clearing your browser's site data removes it, but you then lose the ability to remove your published entries yourself (contact us instead).

Cookies, analytics and other services

  • No advertising or tracking cookies. If you sign in with Discord to upload planner builds, we set one sign-in cookie (bo_s) that keeps you signed in for 30 days, and a short-lived one (bo_st) during sign-in that guards against forged sign-ins. Both are needed for sign-in to work and are removed when you sign out.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site (Cloudflare Pages). Like any web host, its servers process your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and keep the service secure. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Discord handles sign-in if you choose it. You sign in on Discord's own page; we only ask for the "identify" permission (your name, ID and picture). Your profile picture is loaded from Discord's servers.
  • Upstash (a database service) stores what you publish: planner builds, share links, shared filters and star counts (and the closed Builds Ladder's old entries). It only ever receives that published data from our server, never your IP address or anything from your browser.
  • The Broken Oath public API (api.playbrokenoath.com) supplies the Online Ladder. Our server fetches and caches its files, so your browser only talks to this site, and nothing about you is sent to it.
  • Google Fonts provides the site's fonts, so your browser contacts Google to download them, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • Link previews (when you paste a share link into Discord or similar) are generated by our server from the stored summary only.
  • Ko-fi handles donations if you choose to give one, on Ko-fi's own pages, with payment through PayPal or Stripe. We never see your card or bank details. Ko-fi tells us what it shows any creator, such as the name and message you choose to leave. Ko-fi's privacy policy applies there.

Why we process this, and for how long

We only store what you choose to publish, to provide the feature you used (consent and legitimate interest in running the tool you asked for). Published entries stay until you remove them, the site owner removes them (for example spam or abuse), or the site shuts down. We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone beyond the hosting services above.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything you published, and you can object to its use. You can remove your own planner builds and shared filters from the site directly; "Delete my uploads" removes every planner build you uploaded at once. For anything else, including share links, contact us. If you're in South Africa you may also complain to the Information Regulator; in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

If you live in the United States

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising or use it for profiling. We don't collect sensitive personal information. If your state gives you privacy rights (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and others), you can use them through the contact route above: to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it or to delete it. We won't treat you differently for asking. Because nothing is sold or shared for advertising, there is nothing to opt out of, and a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser is respected by default.

Keeping it safe

The site runs only over HTTPS, keeps as little as it can (no passwords, emails or game IDs), scrambles the keys that prove you own an entry, and checks the size and content of everything published. No system is perfectly secure, though, so please don't publish anything you need kept private. If we learn of a breach that puts your information at risk, we'll post a notice on the site and tell the regulators the law requires us to.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not aimed at children. If you are under 13, please don't sign in or publish anything. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has signed in or published something, we'll delete it. Parents can contact us to have it removed.
