Skill Scaling

What each damage skill does as its gem levels up, from the game data of build 95. The top colour is the gem's: red Strength, green Dexterity, blue Intelligence. Attacks show their share of your weapon's damage; spells show the damage of one hit before anything else adds to it.

How gem levels work

A gem levels to 20 by itself. Levels past 20 come from gear and the tree ("+1 to Level of all ... Skill Gems"). For spells every level multiplies damage by ×1.19; the first 5 gear levels count in full and later ones count a little less (×1.1, ×1.07, ×1.04, ×1.02). Attacks gain 8% effectiveness a gem level, and each gear level gives ×1.03 more. Some skills change at late levels on top of that (the game's late gem table): those cards say so in green or red. Numbers here are the skill alone, before supports, the passive tree and gear.
