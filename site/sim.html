Built for the Broken Oath Database from the Broken Oath playtest files (11 Nov 2026 patch, build 68) and the damage formula read from the game's code: gem growth ×1.19 per level, attack effectiveness +8% per level up to 20 and ×1.06 per gear level, gem-scaled added damage ×1.19 per level and ×effectiveness on attacks. Rain and Repeat on a single target follow their descriptions (Rain strikes three times, four with 4+ projectiles, a Ballista twice; Repeat fires again at 40% less) and are the least certain part. Not modelled: Trap and Skyfall timing, damage over time (ignite, poison, bleed), armour against physical, pack damage from chains and extra projectiles. Shock size on bosses and Hunter's Mark at full stacks are assumptions. Fan-made, not affiliated with the developers.
