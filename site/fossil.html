Fossil Planner

Plan the idols in your Dwarven Fossil: 21 cells, a 5 by 5 square without its corners. Idols don't turn, so a 1×2 and a 2×1 are different idols. Add idols from the list, click one in the Fossil to read the mods it can roll, and use Another layout to fit a set that doesn't fit as placed. Your layout is kept in this browser.

Fossil

Click an empty cell, then an idol's +, to place it there; otherwise it goes in the first place it fits.
