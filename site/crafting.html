Vowbinding planner

Plan a Vowbound red: every Vow it can take, the Marks it can move to, and the runes it costs.
Vowbinding: a Vow (hidden cube recipe). Put a red, a rare of the same slot and the runes in the cube. The red takes the rare's finest mod (its best tier) as a Vow. A Vow of the same kind replaces the old one. The first Vow costs a Tier V or red rune, then 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 13, 17, 22, 28 and 36 red runes: a red holds up to 11 Vows (since the 7 November patch). It never takes the rare's Oathsworn line. Each Vow counts for less than the one before: 100%, 85%, 70%, 60%, 50%, 40%, 33%, 27%, 22%, 18%, 15%. The game applies this live in Vow order, so take your strongest Vow first. A + to Level of all Skill Gems Vow loses only half as much (85% becomes 92.5%, 70% becomes 85%), and the level rounds to the nearest whole, so a +2 in the second slot is still +2.
Vowbinding: the Mark (hidden cube recipe). A red, a Core Touched base of the same kind and a Tier V or red rune. The red moves onto that base: every value is kept and the Mark becomes its implicit.

Recipes, costs, mod pools, tiers and weights come from the Broken Oath playtest files (10 November 2026 patch, build 66). Vowbinding: which mod the cube takes, the rune costs and each Vow's share (100/85/70/60/50/40%, confirmed on an in-game tooltip) come from the game's recipe text; the planner shows each Vow at its best roll. How many mods magic and rare items roll was measured from 72 real dropped items. The game's own text confirmed that Echoes come from salvaging, that adding an Echo can turn an item magic or rare, and the Oath Seal. A few rules only exist in the game's code, and are estimated here, marked estimated: Forge Potential growth with item level, how many Echoes a salvaged mod gives, the tier crafted mods land on, and which mods count as a jewel's family.
