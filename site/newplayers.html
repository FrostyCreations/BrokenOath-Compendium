The classes

The game's build guides

Three builds per class from the game's own Guide window: one to start with, one for clearing maps and one for bosses. Open in Build Planner loads the build with its passive tree, Dragonling and skill, so you can follow it point by point. The Boss DPS here is the game's own estimate for the finished build at level 100 with the items it names; the planner starts without items, so it shows less until you add them.

Rules worth knowing

  • Increased and more: all "increased" lines of one stat add into one sum; each "more" line multiplies on its own. A new "more" line is usually worth more than one more "increased" line.
  • Engines: lines like "more damage per 1,000 Armour" or "per 10 Strength" add together first and then multiply once, so a second engine is worth less than the first.
  • Damage taken: every "enemy takes more damage" effect (Woes, Sentences, Hunter's Mark, Shock) adds into one pool, and Guardians take your Woes in full.
  • Gem levels: a gem levels to 20 on its own; "+1 to Level" lines on gear and the tree take it past 20. Skill Scaling shows what each level does.
  • Dragonling: you get a Dragonling point at Rupture level 10, 20 and so on to 100, then at 120, 140, 160, 180 and 200, plus one at hatching. Its Aspect (Fire, Lightning, Cold, Arcane, Poison, Iron or Void) changes the Heart nodes.
  • Oaths: an Oath Mastery seat on the passive tree opens once you take one of the notables next to it; you then swear one of its Oaths.
  • Idols: idols sit in your Dwarven Fossil, 21 cells. Plan them with the Fossil Planner.
