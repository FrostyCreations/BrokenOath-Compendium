Where is my save?

  1. Close the game or go to the character screen, so the save is up to date.
  2. Open the folder for your system, then the folder named with numbers (your Steam ID).
  3. Each character is one BO_Char_….sav file. The newest one is usually the character you just played.

Heralds, Woes and Vows

Click one to swear or cast it. Each shows the mana it reserves and what it adds to your main skill's boss DPS right now. Gem level changes a Herald's extra damage and Vow of Clarity's cost. Since build 68 only skills on your bar work: each Herald, Vow or Golem you count here needs its own bar slot, and minions reserve their upkeep.

Best supports

Tries every combination of supports for with this character's gear, tree, heralds and curses, and lists the top 10. Click a support to cycle it: ✓ always in, ✕ always out, then back to either. Your picks are remembered per skill in this browser.

Suggest nodes

Tries every node within 5 points of your tree and takes the one with the best gain per point (its path included), then repeats until the points run out.

Node notes

Every node's tooltip now says which skills and damage types each modifier works with. This list shows nodes worth a second look: modifiers the in-game text doesn't mention, and modifiers that need tags no skill has. Handy for players, and for the developer to spot nodes that may not work.

Build setup

Bonus passive points

Each Gate (Rupture 10, 20 … 70) drops an Inscribed Quartz, and so do the Guardians at Rupture 42, 45, 48, 52, 55 and 58: +1 passive point each, 13 in all. Setting your deepest Rupture ticks the ones you've passed; untick any you skipped.

Pick the skill and its supports in the Damage panel, and your Heralds, Woes and Vows under it. A blank build has no gear besides a plain weapon, so damage comes from the weapon, gem, tree and supports only.

Defences and resistances

Worked out the way the game builds your sheet: base life per level and Strength, energy shield from Intelligence, evasion from Dexterity, armour from Strength, your items' own armour, evasion and energy shield with their local lines, then every increase. Resistances include the -50% late-game penalty. The evade chance formula is our best reading of the game data.

Max hit

Try a hit

Max hit is the biggest single hit your life plus energy shield takes before you die, after resistances, armour (it holds up to a 1,000 hit, then caps), damage taken lines and evasion glancing.

What each node gives

How much you would lose by refunding each allocated notable and keystone on its own. Small nodes are added up in the last row.

Make an offline save

  1. Close the game. It rewrites your saves when it closes, so it must not be running.
  2. Name the hero.
  4. Pick your save folder. The page adds the hero straight into it, makes a backup of every file it changes first, and you're done.
    Windows%LOCALAPPDATA%\BrokenOath\Saved\SaveGames\
    Linux and Steam Deck~/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/compatdata/5461470/pfx/drive_c/users/steamuser/AppData/Local/BrokenOath/Saved/SaveGames/

    Paste the path in the folder picker's address bar, then open the folder named with numbers (your Steam ID) and choose it. The browser asks once to let the page edit it.

  5. Or pick the files yourself. From the Steam ID folder, choose BO_Account.sav, and BO_Stash_Standard.sav too for a rich stash. You get one zip: copy everything in it into the Steam ID folder and say yes to replacing. Back up the folder first.
  6. Play. Start the game and the hero is in your hero list, offline, with its skills, gear and tree.

Everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded. The hero gets this build's level, tree, Dragonling, gear, idols, runes, skills, supports and potions, and the skill gems it needs. Map progress comes from a finished template hero, not from your other heroes. Saves from the playtest can break with game updates, so keep the backup.
